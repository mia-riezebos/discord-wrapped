Where your ad appears

Ads appear beside the report on screens at least 1480 px wide. They never cover the report, and they don’t appear in share images or printed reports.

Choose your run

For another length, make a custom one-time payment. Custom payments use the 4-week rate and must cover at least 7 days: on tall ads $1 buys 2 days, and on square ads $1 buys 4 days.

Pay a custom amount on GitHub

If a placement is full, your purchase goes into its next free window. Refunds are available on request: email me.

Send your ad after paying

  1. Pay on GitHub.
  2. Email your ad to sponsor@mia.cx: the image or text, the link, the placement, and which of my sites you want it on.
  3. I review every ad before it runs and tell you the start date.

Ad rules

I approve every ad by hand. These ads aren’t allowed:

  • Gambling
  • Crypto schemes
  • Adult content
  • Data brokers
  • Tools that ask for Discord logins or tokens

Visitors’ Discord packages stay in their browsers. Ads never see their messages.
