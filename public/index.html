How do I get my Discord data package?
  1. In Discord, open User Settings → Data & Privacy.
  2. Choose Request all of my Data. Messages is the only part this page needs.
  3. Discord emails a download link, usually within a few days. Download package.zip and drop it here.
  4. Read Discord's data request guide.

Questions about Discord Wrapped

How do I see my Discord stats?

Open your Discord data package in Discord Wrapped. Choose package.zip or the folder you unzipped it into. You'll see message counts, charts, top words, DMs and servers from your sent messages.

Can I search my Discord message history?

Yes. Search for a word or phrase to see matching messages and a chart over time. Discord exports messages you sent, not replies from others. The search covers messages in your saved report.

Does Discord Wrapped upload my messages?

No. Your browser reads the package and builds the report. The package and its messages are never uploaded. If you choose the AI roast, the model downloads and runs in your browser too.

Can I use Discord Wrapped offline?

Yes. It works offline after your first visit: open discordwrapped.app again with Wi-Fi off, or install it as an app from your browser's menu. You can also save the page and open the file. A saved copy uses the standard look. The optional AI roast needs a model download first.

Safari deletes a site's saved data after 7 days of browsing without a visit to it. That includes the offline copy and your saved report.

Does my report stay saved when I close the page?

Yes. Your browser saves the report, so it reopens after a reload or restart. Choose "Update package" to add messages newer than the saved copy. Earlier saved messages stay in the report. Choose "Remove saved data" to clear that browser's copy.

Is Discord Wrapped free?

Yes. I made Discord Wrapped (mia.cx), and it’s free and open source. You can view the source code or sponsor me.

Can I share my Discord Wrapped?

Yes. Select a number, word or highlight, or choose "Share" on a report section. Copy or download the image card. You can also open a post for X, Bluesky, Threads, Mastodon, LinkedIn, Reddit or WhatsApp. Paste or attach the image yourself where needed.
