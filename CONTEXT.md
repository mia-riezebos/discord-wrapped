# Discord Wrapped

Turns someone's Discord data package into a report about their messages, entirely in their browser, and lets them
share pieces of it.

## Language

### The report

**Package**:
The data export someone requests from Discord (package.zip or its unzipped folder). Only its messages are read.
_Avoid_: export, archive, data dump

**Period**:
The span of days the report covers: a week, month or year, all time, or a custom range.
_Avoid_: range, timeframe

**Report**:
Everything shown for one period: sections of numbers, charts and highlights.
_Avoid_: analysis, dashboard

**Section**:
One titled part of the report, such as Overview, Rhythm, or People & servers.

**Highlight**:
A sentence under a chart that states the most notable thing it shows.
_Avoid_: finding, insight, callout

**Share card**:
The image made when someone shares a number, word or highlight.
_Avoid_: screenshot, share image

### Stories

**Story**:
One animated question about the period and its answer, such as "How many messages did you send?" then 58,827.
_Avoid_: slide, card, frame

**Facts**:
The part of a story that picks its data, decides whether there is enough, and supplies its question, answer and share text. Facts are the same in every theme.

**Scene**:
The part of a story that shows its question and answer on screen with motion. A theme replaces scenes, never facts.
_Avoid_: animation, slide

**Reel**:
An ordered run of stories played back to back for a period.
_Avoid_: story (for the whole run), wrapped, slideshow

**Section reel**:
The reel of only one section's stories.

**Guess**:
A story's optional prompt for the person to answer before the reveal, like picking their top word.
_Avoid_: quiz, poll

**Theme**:
The visual and motion language a reel plays in. A year theme can also restyle the report for that year.
_Avoid_: skin, style

**Default theme**:
The theme every reel falls back to.

**Year theme**:
A theme made for one calendar year of data, used when the period is that year. 2021's reel plays in the 2021 theme.
_Avoid_: edition

**Year story**:
One of the one to three extra stories a year theme adds about that year, played after the core stories.

**Core story**:
A story every reel can include, whatever its theme. Year themes can restyle core stories but not remove them.

### Sharing

**Redaction**:
A word, name or quote the person hides from a share card and post before sharing.
_Avoid_: censor, blur
