Discord Unwrapped

See ten years of your Discord messages as charts: when you talk, who you talk to, what you talk about, and the single message you sent most often.

Drop your package.zip here Or choose the zip, or the folder you unzipped it into.
  • Stays on your deviceThis page reads the file itself. Nothing is uploaded, and the page is locked so it can't send your messages anywhere.
  • Works offlineSave this page and open it with Wi-Fi off. It is one HTML file, and it remembers your package until you remove it.
  • Only your sideDiscord exports the messages you sent, not replies from others.
How do I get my package?
  1. In Discord, open User Settings → Data & Privacy.
  2. Choose Request all of my Data. Messages is the only part this page needs.
  3. Discord emails a download link, usually within a few days. Download package.zip and drop it above.
