# discord-wrapped

## 0.4.0

### Minor Changes

- b4b509e: The site now works offline after your first visit, can be installed as an app, and offers a reload when a new version is ready.
- bacaa5b: Rhythm and People & servers now play as stories, each with its own Play button: night owl or early bird, your biggest day, your fastest minute, where your messages went, your top server, who you DMed most and your inner circle.
- 1c71e70: Share images now come in 9:16 for stories as well as 16:9, with 9:16 picked by default on phones.
- b72d5b9: Tap any word on a share card or in its post to hide it, or hide every name or quote at once.
- cc6efb3: Press Play in the period bar to watch your year or All time as a story reel: four Overview stories, each asking a question and revealing your answer, then a summary.
- ecfb20a: The reel now asks you to guess how many messages you sent and how many days you showed up, then shows how close you were and your score at the end.
- 19011c9: After each story in a reel, Share this shares that story's own card, with your guess when you made one. The summary has a Share button for the whole recap.
- 18d349e: Year themes for the years in your package are saved while your browser is idle, so they play offline too.
- d99fc82: Play your Wrapped from the top of the report, play Overview, Words & links or Hall of fame on their own, and share a first message that opens with a link as "a link to youtube.com".
- f0c2065: A year can now have its own theme that restyles its reel, its report and its share cards, and adds stories of its own; every other period keeps the standard look.

### Patch Changes

- 00c1b32: Sponsor ads beside the report are no longer cut off at the top. They sit centred below the bars and stop above the footer.
- a5d10cb: The amber chart colour is a little darker in light mode, so its lines stand out from the page.
- 63b202a: Every platform gets the link to the site in the post itself, so the post shows its preview. X, Threads and LinkedIn no longer move it to a reply.
- e954d7c: Share opens your device's share sheet first where the browser supports it, and the platforms are now tiles with their logos and brand colours.
- cbd86e4: The Sponsor link in the header is easier to read in light mode.
- 7c1d3ec: The report shows mentions, channels and custom emoji in quoted messages as Discord does, and a long name in a story's choices no longer pushes "(your guess)" out of its row.
- 7b2d873: The reel's questions, answers, share posts and guess verdicts read more clearly, keep their tense for All time, and get every plural right.
- f0b8b11: "Share this" in the player now shares the story itself: the image is the story frame as you see it, with the site at the foot. Hide names and tapping a word still black out names on it.
- 2c64136: Stories with a list, chips or bars under the headline no longer draw them over the headline when names or lines run long.
- 1d885dc: Stories and share posts quote messages as Discord shows them, with @someone for mentions and :name: for custom emoji, and every count in a story reads as one or many correctly.
- 43ac4ce: The reel summary shares as its own frame, every answer included. Selecting the text of a chart (its heading, legend or readout) shares that chart instead of the section's main one.

## 0.3.0

### Minor Changes

- d7a203d: Pick the period your report covers: a week, month or year with Previous and Next, all time, or a custom range. Charts, highlights and shares follow the period. Share posts got rewritten copy, and share cards now show the section's chart or the ranked list with your pick highlighted. Every chart has a "Show the numbers" table, bump-chart words work from the keyboard, and highlights are easier to read.

## 0.2.1

### Patch Changes

- d110f8d: The site now also answers at discordwrapped.app and www.discordwrapped.app.
- c68c1b2: The FAQ and Advertise page speak in my own voice instead of calling me Mia.
- 03c8d08: Share posts read like posts now, such as "My first ever message on Discord was “bro”! Check yours on discordwrapped.app". Curly and straight apostrophes count as the same word, so "it's" and "it’s" no longer split in the top words chart, and searching either finds both.

## 0.2.0

### Minor Changes

- 024cf5a: Rename the site to Discord Wrapped. Add a chart of your top 10 words for every year, share cards for any number, word or highlight you select (with per-platform posts and link replies where links get buried), and sponsor rails beside the report on wide screens. Share links and cards use whichever address you opened the site on. The header now shows on every page, with links to the source code, the Discord server and sponsoring, and the site has a proper footer.
- c0027bc: Sidebar ads now come from the shared sponsor service, take turns on either side, and support text ads and mirrored campaigns. Monthly sponsors appear in a footer grid, and a new Advertise page shows placements, prices, next openings and a live preview. Page titles, descriptions and a FAQ help people find the site in search, and the interface copy is clearer.
