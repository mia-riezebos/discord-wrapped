# discord-wrapped

## 0.3.0

### Minor Changes

- d7a203d: Pick the period your report covers: a week, month or year with Previous and Next, all time, or a custom range. Charts, highlights and shares follow the period. Share posts got rewritten copy, and share cards now show the section's chart or the ranked list with your pick highlighted. Every chart has a "Show the numbers" table, bump-chart words work from the keyboard, and highlights are easier to read.
